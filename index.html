Student Talent Showcase — M.Sc. Health Data Science

The MSc Health Data Science programme at SRM prepares graduates at the intersection of healthcare, data, statistics, and technology, with a strong emphasis on learning through real-world application.

Graduates develop proficiency in Python, R, SQL, machine learning, deep learning, Power BI, Tableau, large language models, dashboard development, Electronic Medical Records (EMR), Hospital Information Systems (HIS), and healthcare data analytics.

About the Programme

The programme emphasises practical and experiential learning, hands-on work with real-world healthcare data, research methodology, and structured industry exposure. Students learn data management, analysis, interpretation, visualization, and evidence-based decision-making relevant to healthcare and allied sectors.
